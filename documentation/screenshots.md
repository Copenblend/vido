# Screenshots

← [Back to Home](../README.md)

Capture and save individual frames from your videos as image files.

---

## Enabling Screenshots

Screenshot capture is disabled by default. To enable it:

1. Open **Settings** (click the gear icon in the activity bar)
2. Go to **Screenshot**
3. Turn on **Enable Screenshot Capture**

A **camera button** immediately appears in the title bar toolbar.

---

## Taking a Screenshot

1. Play or pause a video at the frame you want to capture
2. Click the **camera button** in the title bar
3. A shutter sound plays to confirm the capture
4. The image is saved to your screenshot folder

---

## Screenshot Save Directory

By default, screenshots are saved to:

```
%USERPROFILE%\Pictures\Screenshots
```

To change the save location:

1. Open **Settings** → **Screenshot**
2. Set the **Screenshot Save Directory** to your preferred folder

> **Tip:** The save directory setting only appears when screenshot capture is enabled.
